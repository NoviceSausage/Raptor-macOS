release is written by this repository's maintainer.** The packaging scripts are
original; the program itself is not. The notices below exist so that
redistribution stays honest and traceable.

## 1. RAPTOR — the application itself

RAPTOR is a flowchart-based programming environment for teaching algorithmic
reasoning: students build programs visually and trace execution through a
flowchart, learning to program without syntactic baggage.

**It is not a decompiler.** There is a separate, unrelated old freeware .NET
decompiler that also goes by the name "RAPTOR". This repository packages the
flowchart interpreter only.

The build packaged here is the **Avalonia Edition** (multiplatform), which
supersedes the older Windows-only edition.

### Licence — public domain

The official project homepage states, under *Acknowledgements*:

> The UML designer is based on NClass, an open-source UML Class Designer. NClass
> is licensed under the GNU General Public License. **The rest of RAPTOR, by US
> Air Force policy, is public domain.**

So RAPTOR itself carries **no copyright restrictions**: it is a work of the US
federal government produced at the US Air Force Academy, and may be freely
redistributed, modified, and repackaged. The homepage also states that *"RAPTOR
is freely distributed as a service to the CS education community."*

Two caveats, recorded for completeness:

1. **NClass / UML designer.** The GPL-licensed NClass component belongs to the
   **Windows-only** edition's UML designer. This Avalonia Edition does not ship
   it: the packaged payload contains no NClass or UML designer assembly and no
   GPL notice (verified by scanning every assembly and `raptor.deps.json`). The
   GPL therefore does not attach to this bundle.
2. **Third-party libraries** bundled alongside RAPTOR are *not* public domain
   and keep their own licences — see section 2.

Unlike typical public-domain projects, the upstream archive contains **no
`LICENSE`, `COPYING`, or `README` file**. The public-domain determination above
comes from the official homepage, not from the distribution.

### What this repository changes

Nothing inside `raptor.dll` or any other upstream assembly is modified. The
packaging adds a bundle around the untouched upstream files:

- `Contents/MacOS/RAPTOR` — a launcher compiled from `tools/launcher.c`
- `Contents/Info.plist` — generated by `tools/package-macos.sh`
- `Contents/Resources/RAPTOR.icns` — generated by `tools/icon-gen.swift`
- Non-macOS runtime folders and debug symbols are **removed** (see `--no-trim`)
